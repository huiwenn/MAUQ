# From Passive Metric to Active Signal: The Evolving Role of Uncertainty Quantification in Large Language Models

**Authors:** Zhang et al.

**Venue:** arXiv:2601.15690, January 2026

**Category:** Uncertainty Quantification / Survey

## Summary

Comprehensive survey charting the evolution of uncertainty quantification in LLMs from passive diagnostic metric to active control signal for agents and reasoning. Covers the full landscape: token-level entropy, semantic uncertainty, conformal methods, and how UQ is increasingly being used not just to measure reliability but to actively guide agent behavior, routing, and self-correction.

## Key Contribution

Maps the trajectory of UQ from measurement to control -- showing how uncertainty signals are becoming first-class inputs to agent decision-making rather than post-hoc diagnostics.

## Relevance to Gas Town / MAUQ

Essential for positioning MAUQ in the broader literature. MAUQ's use of UQ to predict debate correctness (AUROC 0.9372) is exactly this "active signal" paradigm. The survey provides the framing for arguing that MAUQ represents the frontier of this evolution.
