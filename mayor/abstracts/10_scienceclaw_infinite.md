# ScienceClaw + Infinite: Autonomous Agents Coordinating Distributed Discovery Through Emergent Artifact Exchange

**Authors:** Fiona Y. Wang, Lee Marom, Subhadeep Pal, Rachel K. Luu, Wei Lu, Jaime A. Berkovich, Markus J. Buehler

**Venue:** arXiv:2603.14312, March 15, 2026

**Category:** DAG / Agent Workflows / Scientific Discovery

## Abstract

A framework for autonomous scientific investigation in which independent agents conduct research without central coordination. Features an artifact layer that preserves full computational lineage as a directed acyclic graph (DAG) with typed metadata and parent lineage. Schema-overlap matching triggers multi-parent synthesis across independent analyses. An autonomous mutation layer prunes the expanding artifact DAG to resolve conflicting workflows. The system converts outputs into auditable scientific records through structured posts, provenance views, and machine-readable discourse relations.

## Key Contribution

Introduces "plannerless coordination" where agents produce immutable artifacts with DAG-tracked provenance, enabling decentralized discovery without a central orchestrator. The DAG is the coordination mechanism itself.
