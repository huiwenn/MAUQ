# Large Language Model based Multi-Agents: A Survey of Progress and Challenges

**Authors:** Taicheng Guo, Xiuying Chen, Yaqi Wang, Ruidi Chang, Shichao Pei, Nitesh V. Chawla, Olaf Wiest, Xiangliang Zhang

**Venue:** arXiv:2402.01680, January 21, 2024 (revised April 19, 2024)

**Category:** Multi-Agent Systems / Survey

## Abstract

This comprehensive survey examines LLM-based multi-agent systems and their applications. While individual LLMs serve as autonomous agents for task automation, multi-agent systems have achieved considerable progress in complex problem-solving and world simulation. The survey addresses: what domains and environments LLM-based multi-agents simulate; how agents are profiled and communicate; what mechanisms enhance agent capabilities; and commonly used datasets and benchmarks. The authors maintain an open-source GitHub repository to keep the community informed of emerging research.

## Key Contribution

The most comprehensive early survey of the LLM multi-agent field, providing a structured taxonomy of agent profiling, communication mechanisms, and capability enhancement methods across diverse application domains.
